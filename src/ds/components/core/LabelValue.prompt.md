Label/value pair — the whole of "Rule Details", "Company Information", "Ticket Information". `stack` is the fact-column form used across the top of detail pages.

```jsx
<LabelValue label="Calculation Type" value="Percentage of Job Value" />
<LabelValue layout="stack" label="Charge Amount" value="₦1,050,000.00" hint="₦75,000.00 / day" />
```
